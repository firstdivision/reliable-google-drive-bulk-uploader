Reliable browser uploads

Upload photos and videos to Google Drive.

Send your batch directly from your phone or computer to a Google Drive folder you choose. Follow your progress, pause when you need to, and retry failed files in one place.

Open BatchHarbor

Keep the page open and active until your batch finishes. Background uploading is not supported.

Direct transfer

Selected media goes from your browser to Google Drive over HTTPS. BatchHarbor has no upload server and does not receive a copy.

Stay in control

Pause and resume your batch, track progress confirmed by Google Drive, and retry failed files without restarting completed uploads.

Your files, your folder

Choose photos and videos from your device and a destination in your own Google Drive. Your originals stay on your device.

From your device to your Drive

  1. Select photos and videos using your device’s native picker.
  2. Connect your Google account and choose a destination folder.
  3. Start the batch, keep the page visible, and watch aggregate progress.
  4. Pause, resume, or retry failed files without restarting completed work.

Keep Awake helps keep your screen on where supported. Leave your device plugged in and the page active during long transfers.

After closing or reloading the page, recovery is best-effort: it depends on saved batch records, access to the original files, and the same Google account. Keep your originals and verify uploaded files in Drive.

Your media stays on the path you chose

Connect securely through Google. BatchHarbor requests access only to files and folders you open with it or create through it, not your entire Drive. It does not ask for your Google password, run advertising or analytics, or sell user data.
